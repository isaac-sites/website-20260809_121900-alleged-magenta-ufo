## Introduction

The RS/33 material associated with the alleged 1933 Magenta UFO crash is notable because it does not preserve a single, consistent picture of the object. Alongside the better-known disc descriptions, some [documents]({{ 'documents/' | relative_url }}) and later reproductions connected with the case describe a long, cylindrical or cigar-like vehicle. These sketches and written descriptions are important because they show that the [Magenta narrative]({{ 'narrative-expansion/' | relative_url }}) developed around several competing shapes rather than one fixed design.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rr0.org/science/crypto/ufo/enquete/dossier/Magenta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rr0.org">[RR0]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rr0.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Le crash de Magenta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Le crash de MagentaMay 4, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 4, 2025</span></span></span>

## Reported cylinder-like outlines

### The cigar-shaped craft in RS/33-linked accounts

Among the most distinctive descriptions linked to the RS/33 collection is a report of a cylindrical object observed over northern Italy. The account describes a long, tube-like craft with side openings and coloured lights, giving it a very different appearance from the classic flying saucer associated with many UFO reports. A reproduced sketch connected with this material depicts an elongated vehicle rather than a flat disc.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rr0.org/science/crypto/ufo/enquete/dossier/Magenta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rr0.org">[RR0]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rr0.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Le crash de Magenta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Le crash de MagentaMay 4, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 4, 2025</span></span></span>

This cylindrical description appears in connection with a handwritten memorandum and sketch attributed to the RS/33 document collection. According to later presentations of the material, the drawing accompanied a 1936 account describing an unidentified aircraft seen over Mestre, with features including a cylindrical body, openings along the side, and white and red lights.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://rr0.org/science/crypto/ufo/enquete/dossier/Magenta/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: rr0.org">[RR0]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">rr0.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Le crash de Magenta</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Le crash de MagentaMay 4, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 4, 2025</span></span></span>

The importance of this version is not that it proves a particular craft design existed, but that it demonstrates how varied the alleged documentation is. A reader encountering only the popular “[Mussolini]({{ 'mussolini/' | relative_url }}) UFO” image might expect a saucer, yet the RS/33-associated descriptions include shapes closer to a dirigible, aircraft fuselage, or experimental vehicle.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>

### Why the cylinder description stands apart

The cylindrical form occupies an unusual position in the Magenta story because it sits between familiar aviation shapes and later UFO imagery. A tube-shaped object with lights and openings could be interpreted in many ways: an [unknown aircraft]({{ 'unknown-aircraft/' | relative_url }}), a misidentified conventional vehicle, an imagined futuristic machine, or, within UFO interpretations, an advanced craft. The description itself does not resolve which explanation is correct.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>

This ambiguity is central to the RS/33 material. The documents do not provide a complete technical specification, manufacturing record, or independently confirmed chain of custody for an object. Instead, they offer descriptions and sketches whose meaning depends heavily on how the wider Magenta narrative is interpreted.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

## Sketches linked to RS/33 material

### The role of drawings in shaping the case

The sketches associated with the alleged RS/33 papers have had an outsized influence because they give a visual form to otherwise uncertain descriptions. A written report of an “unknown aircraft” can remain abstract, but a drawing immediately encourages comparison with known UFO categories such as discs, cylinders, and cigar-shaped craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

The available material is usually discussed as containing several types of imagery: descriptions of a metallic disc with portholes, references to cigar-like or cylindrical forms, and sketches accompanying reports of unidentified aerial objects. These variations are one reason the Magenta case has produced different visual representations over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.coloradomufon.org/the-magenta-case-italys-1933-ufo-mystery/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: coloradomufon.org">[Colorado MUFON]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">coloradomufon.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta case italys 1933 ufo mystery</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Colorado MUFONThe Magenta Case: First Reported UFO Crash – Colorado MUFONMay 5, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 5, 2026</span></span></span>

The cylinder sketches also illustrate a wider issue in historical UFO documentation: drawings are rarely neutral records. They are interpretations made by observers, investigators, translators, or later publishers. A sketch may preserve an original impression, but it may also reflect the expectations and technology concepts of the period in which it was produced or circulated.

### The gap between a sketch and physical evidence

A key distinction in evaluating RS/33 sketches is the difference between documentary evidence and material evidence. The sketches are evidence that certain descriptions circulated within the Magenta story, but they are not the same as verified [photographs]({{ 'photographs/' | relative_url }}), recovered components, or independently examined artefacts.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

Supporters of the story may point to the variety of descriptions as evidence that different sightings involved related unknown technology. Critics may instead see the changing shapes as a sign that the narrative accumulated details from different sources over time.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.metabunk.org/threads/claim-a-ufo-crashed-in-1933-italy-and-the-us-recovered-it.13282/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: metabunk.org">[Metabunk]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">metabunk.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">claim a ufo crashed in 1933 italy and the us recovered it.13282</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Claim: A UFO crashed in 1933 Italy and the US Recovered it &#124; Metabunk...</span></span></span>
