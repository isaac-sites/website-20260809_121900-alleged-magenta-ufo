## Introduction

The idea that [Gabinetto]({{ 'rs-33/' | relative_url }}) RS/33 was a **reverse-engineering programme** is not the clearest or earliest layer of the [alleged Magenta UFO]({{ 'alleged-magenta-ufo/' | relative_url }}) story. In the material publicised by Italian researchers Roberto Pinotti and Alfredo Lissoni, RS/33 is first presented mainly as a secret body concerned with unidentified or “non-conventional” aircraft: collecting reports, assessing whether unfamiliar machines belonged to foreign powers, coordinating investigations and suppressing sensitive information. Pinotti’s English-language account describes Mussolini as initially suspecting French, British or German secret aircraft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>



<figure class="article-media-figure article-media-figure-illustration"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_rs33_reverse_enginee_46cad1-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Mission Drift illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_rs33_reverse_enginee_46cad1-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_rs33_reverse_enginee_46cad1-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><figcaption class="article-media-caption"><span class="article-media-caption-label">Explanatory illustration 1</span></figcaption></figure>

The stronger claim — that scientists under Guglielmo Marconi tried to **extract and reproduce technology from a recovered craft** — becomes much more explicit in later interpretations. By 2023, Italy’s Centro Ufologico Nazionale (CUN) was describing Marconi as having conducted unsuccessful “reverse engineering studies” intended to replicate the recovered vehicle’s technology.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.eu/rivelazioni-di-david-grusch-sui-file-fascisti-comunicato-stampa-cun/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.eu">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Rivelazioni di David Grusch sui file fascisti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleRivelazioni di David Grusch sui file fascisti - Comunicato Stampa CUN - CENTRO UFOLOGICO NAZIONALE...</span></span></span>

That shift matters. It turns RS/33 from an alleged intelligence-and-investigation office into something resembling a modern crash-retrieval exploitation programme. Yet the surviving published material does not provide a comparable trail of laboratory reports, engineering drawings, test results or component analyses demonstrating that such work occurred.

## What the earlier RS/33 accounts say the group did

The earlier published RS/33 narrative is centred much more firmly on **identification and intelligence** than on technological replication. In [Pinotti]({{ 'pinotti/' | relative_url }})’s account of the alleged files, the Magenta object is an “unconventional flying vehicle”, but [Mussolini]({{ 'mussolini/' | relative_url }}) supposedly thought it might be an advanced secret weapon belonging to another European state. The wider body of alleged RS/33 material then concerns sightings between 1933 and 1940, including the well-known account of unusual objects over Mestre and Venice in 1936.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

That distinction is easy to lose because modern UFO vocabulary encourages readers to translate “secret investigation of a recovered [unknown aircraft]({{ 'unknown-aircraft/' | relative_url }})” almost automatically into “reverse engineering”. The alleged [documents]({{ 'documents/' | relative_url }}) themselves, however, are associated more visibly with activities such as reporting sightings and controlling information. One purported Stefani news-agency message orders “absolute secrecy” concerning unidentified aircraft, while Pinotti describes RS/33 as linked to the Fascist political police and the regime's information machinery.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

The same source says a lost dossier contained roughly 30 pages concerning these unconventional vehicles. Pinotti also reports finding hundreds of genuine-era prefectural telegrams concerning aerial observations, although the existence of authentic contemporary reports of unidentified aircraft would not by itself authenticate RS/33 or the Magenta recovery narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

Most importantly for the reverse-engineering question, this early picture does not produce the sort of documentation expected from an engineering exploitation programme. There is no publicly established RS/33 technical archive showing, for example, a recovered propulsion unit being dismantled, materials undergoing laboratory analysis, dimensions being copied into experimental hardware or engineers recording failed attempts to reproduce a component. Even Pinotti's presentation moves from documentary claims about unidentified aircraft towards technological exploitation largely through inference.

This is why the wording matters. **“Study the problem” and “study the vehicle” are not equivalent to “reverse-engineer extraterrestrial technology”.** An intelligence service examining an unfamiliar aircraft to determine its origin and capabilities would be doing something entirely conventional. Reverse engineering requires the stronger proposition that investigators possessed hardware and deliberately attempted to reproduce its operating principles.

## How propulsion and weapons entered the story

The expansion becomes especially visible when later writers connect RS/33 with genuine Italian aeronautical developments. Pinotti asks whether Italy's work on jet propulsion might have resulted from studying the supposed Magenta object. He points to Secondo Campini's work and the Caproni Campini experimental aircraft, then explicitly raises the question of whether this development had anything to do with reverse engineering the unknown machines described in the alleged Fascist files.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

But the chronology actually weakens that inference. Pinotti himself notes that Campini had proposed jet propulsion in **1932**, before the alleged 1933 Magenta recovery. These examples reveal an important mechanism in the legend's growth: **real experimental technology becomes retrospective circumstantial evidence for an alleged secret programme**. Jet aircraft, circular designs and unconventional propulsion are historically interesting in their own right. Once RS/33 is assumed to have possessed an extraordinary craft, however, technologies developed afterwards can be reread as possible products of that craft — even when ordinary developmental histories already exist.

### Marconi's “death ray” became the strongest bridge

The most influential bridge between investigation and reverse engineering is probably the legend of Marconi's so-called [death ray]({{ 'death-ray/' | relative_url }}). In an older RS/33 discussion, Lissoni explicitly presented the connection as speculation: because Marconi was reputed to have developed a device capable of disabling engines, his alleged involvement in a UFO commission raised the possibility that Fascist researchers were studying alien technology. Crucially, the argument was framed there as a hypothesis rather than a documented RS/33 achievement.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ecn.org/cunfi/rs33.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ecn.org">[ECN]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ecn.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il Gabinetto RS-33</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il Gabinetto RS-33...</span></span></span>

There is a much less exotic historical explanation for the stories surrounding Marconi. He genuinely investigated the use of radio waves to detect moving objects. Experiments at the radio research centre at Torre Chiaruccia involved directing electromagnetic transmissions towards objects including aircraft and vehicles and studying reflected signals — work related to the development of radio-location technology. Wired's historical examination notes that such experiments could readily have helped generate stories that Marconi possessed a beam capable of stopping engines. It also notes that Marconi denied developing the supposed superweapon and that no evidence establishing its existence has emerged.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.it/play/cultura/2018/04/28/mussolini-leggenda-raggio-morte/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.it">[Wired Italia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Italia Mussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">aliaMussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia...</span></span></span>

The “death ray” therefore illustrates how the RS/33 narrative can grow without a new primary document. Two stories that are individually uncertain — Marconi's miraculous weapon and Marconi's leadership of a UFO programme — reinforce one another when combined. The weapon becomes evidence that reverse engineering occurred; RS/33, in turn, becomes an explanation for where the mysterious weapon supposedly came from.

By the late 2010s, this fusion was sufficiently established that Wired could describe the UFO version of the death-ray legend specifically as a claim that Marconi's weapon represented alien reverse engineering. The article also quotes Italian UFO researcher Giuseppe Stilo arguing that, from around 1999 onwards, the Mussolini-UFO narrative had expanded far beyond the proposition that an unusual object simply crashed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.it/play/cultura/2018/04/28/mussolini-leggenda-raggio-morte/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.it">[Wired Italia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Italia Mussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">aliaMussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia...</span></span></span>

## Why changing language matters when judging the legend

The evolution from **investigation** to **reverse engineering** is not merely stylistic. Each description carries a different evidential burden.

An alleged document ordering secrecy about an unknown aircraft can support, at most, a claim that somebody wanted information controlled — assuming the document itself is authentic. A report describing an unidentified object can support a claim that somebody recorded an unusual observation. Neither automatically demonstrates possession of a vehicle.

Possession is another step. Establishing reverse engineering requires another step beyond that: evidence that specialists systematically analysed the recovered technology with the aim of reproducing it. Claims that successful weapons or propulsion advances resulted from the programme require still more evidence connecting specific RS/33 work to those technologies.

The published RS/33 story repeatedly crosses these boundaries through inference. For example:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* alleged reports of unfamiliar aircraft become evidence for an organised RS/33 investigation;
* an alleged recovered vehicle becomes the presumed object of technical study;
* Marconi's real electromagnetic research is associated with the legendary death ray;
* the death-ray legend is then reinterpreted as a possible product of alien reverse engineering;
* genuine Italian jet and unconventional-aircraft experiments become candidates for technological “spin-offs”;
* later retellings condense these possibilities into the statement that RS/33 **was** a reverse-engineering programme.

</div>

This does not prove that every later author deliberately embellished the story. Legends often develop through cumulative interpretation: a speculative question in one account becomes a possibility in another, then background information in a third. Once the phrase “reverse engineering” is attached to RS/33, it also supplies a familiar modern framework through which earlier, vaguer material can be reread.

There is a useful internal warning sign in Pinotti's own material. His 2020 presentation asks whether Italian jet development or Belluzzo's circular design might have resulted from studying the alleged craft, rather than presenting RS/33 engineering records that demonstrate such a transfer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span> That is fundamentally different from CUN's later categorical description of Marconi conducting unsuccessful reverse-engineering studies.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.centroufologiconazionale.eu/rivelazioni-di-david-grusch-sui-file-fascisti-comunicato-stampa-cun/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: centroufologiconazionale.eu">[Centro Ufologico Nazionale]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">centroufologiconazionale.eu</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Centro Ufologico Nazionale Rivelazioni di David Grusch sui file fascisti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Centro Ufologico NazionaleRivelazioni di David Grusch sui file fascisti - Comunicato Stampa CUN - CENTRO UFOLOGICO NAZIONALE...</span></span></span>

## What would actually establish reverse engineering?

For the reverse-engineering version of RS/33 to move beyond an expanded interpretation of the Magenta legend, evidence would need to connect the alleged recovered object directly to an engineering programme. The strongest material would be contemporary, independently authenticated records with a traceable archival origin: laboratory notebooks, component inventories, metallurgical reports, technical drawings, budgets, correspondence assigning specific experiments, [photographs]({{ 'photographs/' | relative_url }}) with reliable provenance or engineering records describing attempts to reproduce a recovered system.

That evidence gap is particularly important because the claimed documentary foundation is already contested. Pinotti says forensic examination authenticated important papers as Fascist-era material and reports that a historian found their language, stationery and terminology consistent with the period.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span> Critics counter that the papers lack a demonstrated government-archive provenance and that the underlying forensic work has not been sufficiently available for independent checking.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.pepijnvanerp.nl/2023/06/mussolinis-ufo-cabinet-rs-33/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: pepijnvanerp.nl">[Pepijn van Erp]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">pepijnvanerp.nl</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pepijn van Erp Mussolini’s UFO – Cabinet RS/33 – Pepijn van Erp</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Pepijn van Erp Mussolini’s UFO – Cabinet RS/33 – Pepijn van Erp</span></span></span> Dating paper or ink to the 1930s would in any case address the age of a physical document, not automatically prove every extraordinary event described on it.

The technological claims face an additional problem: known historical developments provide independent explanations for the inventions later folded into the story. Marconi's electromagnetic experiments fit the development of radio-location techniques without requiring recovered extraterrestrial hardware.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.wired.it/play/cultura/2018/04/28/mussolini-leggenda-raggio-morte/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: wired.it">[Wired Italia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">wired.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Italia Mussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">aliaMussolini e la leggenda del raggio della morte &#124; Wired Italia...</span></span></span> Campini's propulsion work predates the alleged Magenta crash.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[The Black Vault]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">The Black Vault THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Black VaultTHE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span> Belluzzo's circular concepts likewise belong to a recognisable terrestrial history of experimental wartime aviation.

The most careful description of the evidence is therefore that **reverse engineering represents a later, stronger layer of the RS/33 narrative**. Early accounts establish — within the disputed documents' own story — a secret organisation investigating unidentified aircraft and controlling information. Subsequent interpretations connect that organisation with Marconi's technological work, speculative weapons and unconventional aviation. Eventually, those connections are compressed into the claim that RS/33 attempted to reproduce recovered non-human technology.

That progression makes the reverse-engineering story historically interesting, but it also makes its wording diagnostically important. The further a retelling moves from “investigated unidentified aircraft” towards “replicated alien propulsion and weapons”, the more evidence it requires — and the less support is currently available from independently authenticated, contemporary engineering records.
