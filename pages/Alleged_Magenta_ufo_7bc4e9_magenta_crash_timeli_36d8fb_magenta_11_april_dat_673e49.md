## Introduction

The **11 April 1933** date is not merely an internet-era alternative attached to the [alleged Magenta UFO]({{ 'alleged-magenta-ufo/' | relative_url }}) crash. It appears in material published under the name of Roberto Pinotti, one of the principal researchers responsible for bringing the supposed Fascist-era files to public attention. Yet the same Pinotti account also identifies **[13 June]({{ '13-june/' | relative_url }}) 1933** as the date on which the unidentified craft was recovered. That internal contradiction is the central problem.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

The balance of the available documentary trail favours **13 June**, at least as the date claimed by the alleged [RS/33]({{ 'rs-33/' | relative_url }}) papers: the surviving descriptions of the first document batch point to telegrams dated 13 June, and Italian press coverage from April 2000 explicitly gave 13 June as the incident date. Intriguingly, that press coverage itself was datelined **11 April 2000**. This creates a plausible route by which “11 April” could have entered or contaminated later retellings, although no source found establishes that this is what actually happened.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

## How 11 April entered published accounts

The strongest reason to take the April date seriously as a feature of the *story's transmission* is [Pinotti]({{ 'pinotti/' | relative_url }})'s own English-language account. A version reproduced by The Black Vault from his book *UFO Contacts in Italy: Volume Two* says that Pinotti and Alfredo Lissoni presented the material at the 2000 World UFO Symposium in San Marino. It then describes the material as concerning a supposed flying saucer that had crashed or landed [near Magenta]({{ 'near-magenta/' | relative_url }}) on **11 April 1933**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

This is considerably more important than finding 11 April on a later UFO website. The statement is attributed directly to a central investigator and is presented as his retrospective account of the research he and Lissoni had conducted. It demonstrates that the April date belongs somewhere within the Pinotti-Lissoni publication tradition rather than being solely a recent transcription error introduced by outsiders. The same wording has subsequently been reproduced elsewhere, helping 11 April propagate through English-language discussions of Magenta.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.patreon.com/theblackvault/posts/ufo-files-of-ufo-40451201?l=it" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: patreon.com">[Patreon]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">patreon.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti &#124; Patreon</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti &#124; PatreonAugust 14, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 14, 2020</span></span></span>

There is, however, an extraordinary complication. Only a few paragraphs later, **the same Pinotti text changes the date**. Discussing the sequence of purported Italian UFO incidents, it calls the Magenta event the second case and says that on **13 June 1933** a landed UFO was recovered. Pinotti immediately connects that recovery with the telegrams supposedly concerning the craft. In other words, this is not simply a disagreement between a Pinotti account and a sceptical account: **11 April and 13 June coexist within one published narrative attributed to Pinotti himself**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

That makes attempts to treat 11 April as an independently established second chronology difficult. The available account does not explain that there were two events, nor does it say that a crash occurred in April and government action followed in June. Instead, both dates are used for what appears to be the same alleged Magenta incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

## Why 11 April conflicts with the June documents

The case for **13 June 1933** is stronger within the alleged documentary record because that date is attached to the papers around which the original Magenta claim was constructed. A modern inventory by the New Paradigm Institute describes the first batch received in January 1996 as two letters on Italian Senate stationery and three highly confidential telegrams from the Milan telegraphic office, **all dated 13 June 1933**. The institute accepts that the papers remain disputed and that independent verification of the reported forensic work is lacking.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

Pinotti's own account reinforces the June chronology. After giving 11 April near its beginning, he later says the recovery occurred on 13 June and describes several telegrams relating to it. ## Could 11 April have come from April 2000?

One detail deserves particular attention because it offers a mundane possible mechanism for the discrepancy. Major Italian publicity about the alleged Fascist UFO files appeared in **April 2000**, and at least one surviving version of the relevant newspaper story begins with the dateline **“MILANO, 11 APRILE”** — Milan, 11 April. The article then states that the historical incident occurred on **13 June 1933**.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.universonline.it/_misteri/articoli_u/articoli/00_04_18_a.php" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: universonline.it">[Universo On Line]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">universonline.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Universo On Line Ufologia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Universo On LineUfologia - E Mussolini nascose l&#x27;Ufo di Milano...</span></span></span>

That juxtaposition is striking:

**11 April 2000 — publication/dateline**

**13 June 1933 — alleged incident**

It creates an obvious opportunity for the day and month of the modern article to become attached accidentally to the year of the historical claim during later transcription, translation, editing or compilation. Italian reference material also lists articles on the Mussolini-UFO story dated **11 April 2000**, while identifying the alleged historical crash as occurring on 13 June 1933.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/UFO_crash" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">UFO crash</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UFO crash</span></span></span>

This should nevertheless remain a **hypothesis, not a finding**. No discovered statement from Pinotti, Lissoni, an editor or publisher says that “11 April 1933” resulted from confusing an April 2000 publication date with the June 1933 incident date. The coincidence is suggestive because it offers a simple textual pathway, but without the underlying manuscript and editing history it cannot demonstrate how the error arose.

Another possibility is an ordinary authorial or editorial mistake unrelated to the newspaper date. Pinotti's English text is internally inconsistent on its face: it says 11 April near the beginning and 13 June shortly afterwards without acknowledging any disagreement. That pattern is at least compatible with an unnoticed editing or transcription error.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

A third possibility — that the researchers possessed additional information placing the physical event in April while the telegrams documented later official action — cannot be completely excluded. But the published material located here does not supply the [missing evidence]({{ 'missing-evidence/' | relative_url }}) needed to support it. More importantly, Pinotti's explicit description of a UFO being recovered on 13 June works against such a reconstruction.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

## Which date should the Magenta timeline use?

For a historical timeline of the **alleged** Magenta crash, **13 June 1933 is the better-supported conventional date**, with an explicit qualification that the underlying documents themselves remain unverified as authentic government records. It is the date connected with the first alleged telegram batch, the date given in the early Italian press reconstruction, and — crucially — the recovery date stated later in Pinotti's own English account.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[newparadigminstitute.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

**11 April 1933 should not be silently discarded**, however. It belongs in the chronology as a genuine source-history problem because Pinotti explicitly published it in connection with the Magenta crash or landing. What cannot presently be justified is elevating it to equal documentary status with 13 June. No April-dated crash document has emerged in the accessible material examined here, and Pinotti's own narrative does not explain why the two dates differ.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

The safest formulation is consequently that **the Pinotti-Lissoni tradition contains an unresolved date contradiction**. Its purported documentary core points to 13 June 1933, while a later account published under Pinotti's name gives 11 April before reverting, within the same text, to 13 June. The coincidence that Italian publicity for the case was datelined 11 April 2000 offers a plausible explanation for how the April date might have arisen, but it remains unproven.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.theblackvault.com/casefiles/the-ufo-files-of-mussolini-fascist-ufo-files-by-roberto-pinotti/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theblackvault.com">[theblackvault.com]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theblackvault.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">TH E UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">THE UFO FILES OF MUSSOLINI: Fascist UFO Files by Roberto Pinotti - The Black Vault Case Files...</span></span></span>

That small chronological inconsistency has outsized significance for the Magenta case. With no independently established contemporary crash record available to settle the question, dates must be reconstructed from the very source tradition whose reliability is being assessed. Until an earlier publication, manuscript, archival original or explanation from the researchers establishes otherwise, **13 June is the stronger documentary date; 11 April is an important but unexplained competing date within the modern telling of the story**.
