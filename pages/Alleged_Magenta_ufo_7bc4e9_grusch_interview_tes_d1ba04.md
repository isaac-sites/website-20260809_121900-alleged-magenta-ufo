## Introduction

David Grusch’s public comments about the alleged 1933 Magenta UFO crash became one of the most specific claims associated with the modern UAP debate. ## What he said in interviews

### The Magenta claim was presented as a specific historical case

In his 2023 interview with journalist Ross Coulthart on NewsNation, Grusch described the alleged Magenta incident as an early recovery of a non-human craft. ### The interview created a narrower testable claim

The importance of the interview statements is that they turned a broad allegation — that governments may possess recovered unusual technology — into a specific historical proposition: that a particular object was recovered in northern Italy in 1933 and later entered a secret chain of custody. So far, publicly available evidence has not produced an independently verified chain of custody for such an object. The [Magenta narrative]({{ 'narrative-expansion/' | relative_url }}) remains dependent largely on later documents and testimony about those documents rather than publicly authenticated material evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>

## What he said at the hearing

### The sworn testimony focused on a wider crash-retrieval allegation

On 26 July 2023, Grusch testified before the House Oversight Subcommittee on National Security, the Border, and Foreign Affairs. His written statement and oral testimony centred on his allegation that the US government had a long-running UAP crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering effort. He said his knowledge came from his official duties, interviews with individuals, and information gathered during his work involving UAP analysis.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2023</span></span></span>

During the hearing, Grusch made broader claims about recovered craft and biological material, but he did not publicly walk lawmakers through the Magenta case as a standalone piece of evidence. The hearing record therefore differs from the interview record: the sworn testimony established that he was making allegations about a wider programme, not that Congress had received proof of the 1933 Italian event itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2023</span></span></span>

### The hearing did not resolve the Magenta evidence question

The congressional setting increased the seriousness of Grusch’s allegations because false statements under oath carry legal consequences. However, the evidentiary question remained separate: whether the information behind the claims could be independently verified.

The hearing provided lawmakers and the public with Grusch’s account of alleged government knowledge, but it did not produce publicly available physical evidence of a recovered craft, authenticated Magenta documentation, or a confirmed historical record showing that the Italian incident occurred as described.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2023</span></span></span>

## Why the distinction matters

### An interview detail is not the same as verified evidence

The main difference between the interview claims and the sworn testimony is not that Grusch necessarily changed his story. Rather, the two settings served different purposes.

* **Interviews:** Grusch offered a detailed narrative about Magenta, including the alleged location, historical [timeline]({{ 'timeline/' | relative_url }}), and transfer of information.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>
* **Sworn testimony:** Grusch presented a broader allegation that the US government had information about recovered non-human craft and related programmes, while relying largely on information obtained from other people rather than publicly demonstrated physical evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2023</span></span></span>

The distinction is important because a specific historical claim requires specific historical proof. Testimony that a source, document, or briefing exists is different from publicly examining the source itself.

### The credibility debate centres on verification, not only wording

Supporters of Grusch argue that his intelligence background, whistleblower status, and willingness to testify under oath make his allegations worthy of investigation. Critics argue that extraordinary claims require independently accessible evidence, especially when the claims involve a decades-old event with no publicly confirmed artefacts or archival record.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 2023</span></span></span>

The US government’s All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) later reported that it found no verifiable evidence that the US government or private companies possessed extraterrestrial technology or had conducted reverse engineering of such technology. AARO’s conclusions directly challenge the broader crash-retrieval narrative, although they do not function as a direct historical investigation proving or disproving every individual claim about every alleged incident.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.defense.gov/2024/Mar/08/2003409233/-1/-1/0/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Department of War AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of War AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3700894/statement-by-pentagon-press-secretary-maj-gen-pat-ryder-on-the-historical-recor/)

For the Magenta case specifically, the unresolved issue is therefore not whether Grusch mentioned it — he clearly did — but whether the underlying evidence can be independently confirmed. The interview supplied the most detailed public version of the claim; the hearing placed it within a broader sworn allegation about UAP programmes; neither, by itself, established the alleged 1933 recovery as a verified historical event.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newparadigminstitute.org/learn/library/case-study-magenta-italy-1933/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newparadigminstitute.org">[New Paradigm Institute]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newparadigminstitute.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">case study magenta italy 1933</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">New Paradigm InstituteCase Study Magenta Italy 1933 – New Paradigm InstituteAugust 11, 2025...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 11, 2025</span></span></span>
