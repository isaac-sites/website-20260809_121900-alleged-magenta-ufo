## Introduction

The claim that Pope [Pius XII]({{ 'pius-xii/' | relative_url }}) passed information about the alleged 1933 Magenta UFO recovery to the United States is one of the most specific and controversial parts of the wider Magenta story. The allegation is not that the Vatican merely knew about an unusual event, but that Pius XII personally helped create a secret communication route between Italy and American officials before the end of the Second World War. Publicly available evidence, however, has not yet produced a verified Vatican document, US intelligence record, or diplomatic exchange showing that such a transfer occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.usccb.org/news/2024/angels-or-aliens-some-researchers-say-vatican-archives-hold-ufo-secrets" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: usccb.org">[USCCB]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">usccb.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">angels or aliens some researchers say vatican archives hold ufo secrets</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Angels or aliens? ## Pius XII’s diplomatic role before and during the war

### A pope with genuine international access

The plausibility argument behind the backchannel claim begins with a real historical fact: Eugenio Pacelli, later Pope Pius XII, was one of the Vatican’s most experienced diplomats before becoming pope in 1939. He served as papal nuncio to Germany from 1917 to 1929 and later as Vatican secretary of state under Pope Pius XI, giving him extensive experience with European governments and international negotiations.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vaticannews.va/en/vatican-city/news/2024-10/pope-pius-xii-holocaust-jews-rome-peace.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vaticannews.va">[Vatican News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vaticannews.va</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Vatican News Legacy of Pope Pius XII lives on 66 years after his death</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican NewsLegacy of Pope Pius XII lives on 66 years after his death - Vatican NewsOctober 9, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 9, 2024</span></span></span>

During the Second World War, the Holy See maintained diplomatic relationships and received information from governments, religious figures, and humanitarian networks. The Vatican’s wartime communications were often discreet because of its neutral status and the danger faced by people involved in occupied Europe. Newly opened [Vatican archives]({{ 'archive-search/' | relative_url }}) from the Pius XII period contain large collections of diplomatic correspondence, reports, and administrative records covering these activities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.vaticannews.va/en/vatican-city/news/2020-03/holy-see-archives-pope-pius-xii-world-war-ii.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vaticannews.va">[Vatican News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vaticannews.va</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Vatican News Vatican opens archives on Pope Pius XII</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Vatican NewsVatican opens archives on Pope Pius XII - Vatican NewsMarch 2, 2020...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: March 2, 2020</span></span></span>

This background is important because it explains why supporters of the Magenta claim view the Vatican as a possible intermediary. A neutral institution with diplomatic access could theoretically have passed sensitive information between governments. But historical capability is not evidence of a particular event. The existence of Vatican channels does not demonstrate that one of those channels carried information about an alleged recovered craft.

### The difference between diplomatic possibility and documented action

The strongest argument supporting the Pius XII claim is therefore circumstantial. The Vatican had access, Pacelli had political experience, and the wartime period encouraged unofficial communication. These facts establish a setting in which a secret message would not have been impossible.

The missing element is a traceable chain of evidence. A confirmed backchannel would normally be expected to leave some documentary footprint: correspondence, meeting notes, intelligence summaries, diplomatic cables, archival references, or later official records identifying the participants and subject. The publicly available material connected to the Magenta claim has not produced such documentation.

This distinction matters because wartime diplomacy frequently involved confidential exchanges, but historians evaluate individual claims through surviving evidence rather than through the general reputation or capabilities of the people involved.

## The alleged Vatican to US information channel

### What the claim says happened

According to David Grusch’s public statements, the alleged Magenta recovery occurred in Italy in 1933 under Benito [Mussolini]({{ 'mussolini/' | relative_url }})’s government. He claimed that the object was retained by Italian authorities and that, after the collapse of Fascist Italy, Pope Pius XII helped pass knowledge of the event to the United States.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://podscripts.co/podcasts/the-joe-rogan-experience/2065-david-grusch" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: podscripts.co">[PodScripts]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">podscripts.co</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Pod Scripts The Joe Rogan Experience</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The Joe Rogan Experience - #2065 - David Grusch Transcript and DiscussionNovember 21, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: November 21, 2023</span></span></span>

The significance of the Vatican element is that it changes the proposed [timeline]({{ 'timeline/' | relative_url }}). Instead of the United States discovering an unknown Italian secret through military intelligence alone, the story suggests that the Holy See acted as an intermediary between a collapsing European regime and American officials.

This version of events depends on several separate claims all being true:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* that the Magenta recovery itself occurred as described;
* that Italian authorities retained and studied material from it;
* that the Vatican gained knowledge of this material;
* that Pius XII personally authorised or facilitated communication;
* that US officials received and acted upon the information.

</div>

Each step requires independent support. A weakness in any link affects the entire chain.

### Why the “backchannel” wording creates a verification problem

The term “backchannel” can describe informal diplomatic communication, but it also creates an evidentiary challenge. Secret exchanges can leave fewer public records than formal agreements, especially during wartime. However, secrecy does not mean complete absence of evidence.

The Vatican archives opened material from Pius XII’s pontificate in 2020, including extensive records from the Secretariat of State and other Holy See offices.     params.push('toolid=' + encodeURIComponent(cfg.tool_id || '10001'));
    if (reference) params.push('customid=' + encodeURIComponent(reference).slice(0, 256));
    return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId)< 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket)< 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
