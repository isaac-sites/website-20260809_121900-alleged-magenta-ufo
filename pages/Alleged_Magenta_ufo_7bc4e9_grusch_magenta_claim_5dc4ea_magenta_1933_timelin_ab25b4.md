## Introduction

The 1933 Magenta recovery story is the alleged timeline behind one of the oldest modern UFO crash-retrieval narratives. According to later accounts, an unidentified object was recovered [near Magenta]({{ 'near-magenta/' | relative_url }}), Italy, during Benito [Mussolini]({{ 'mussolini/' | relative_url }})’s rule, studied by Italian authorities, and eventually transferred to the United States near the end of the Second World War. The story became internationally prominent in 2023 after former US intelligence official David Grusch cited Magenta as what he described as the earliest European recovery of a partially intact non-human craft.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catholic.org/news/technology/story.php?id=86465" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catholic.org">[Catholic Online]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catholic.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Catholic OnlineIs the Vatican Hiding Evidence of an Alien Crash from 1933? - Technology - News - Catholic OnlineAugust 4, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 4, 2023</span></span></span>

The central difficulty is that the [timeline]({{ 'timeline/' | relative_url }}) exists mainly through later UFO researchers’ interpretations, anonymous [documents]({{ 'documents/' | relative_url }}) reportedly circulated decades after the alleged event, and modern testimony. Publicly available historical records have not established that a crashed non-human craft was recovered in Magenta, that a secret Italian programme studied such an object, or that the United States obtained one during the war.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapdisclosurereads.com/the-magenta-ufo-case-italys-alleged-1933-crash-retrieval-explained/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapdisclosurereads.com">[UAP Disclosure Reads]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapdisclosurereads.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta ufo case italys alleged 1933 crash retrieval explained</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Disclosure ReadsThe Magenta UFO Case: Italy&#x27;s Alleged 1933 Crash Retrieval, Explained - UAP Disclosure ReadsJuly 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 3, 2026</span></span></span> The timeline is therefore best understood as a disputed historical claim: a sequence of alleged events with identifiable milestones, but with major gaps in independent verification.

## The reported Italian recovery sequence

### 1933: The alleged Magenta incident

The story places the beginning of the case in June 1933 near Magenta, a town west of Milan in northern Italy. Later UFO accounts describe an unusual object being recovered after coming down in the area, with the Italian authorities allegedly securing the site and removing the object for examination. Some versions describe a metallic craft with unusual features, although these details come from later retellings rather than independently confirmed 1933 documentation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapdisclosurereads.com/the-magenta-ufo-case-italys-alleged-1933-crash-retrieval-explained/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapdisclosurereads.com">[UAP Disclosure Reads]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapdisclosurereads.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta ufo case italys alleged 1933 crash retrieval explained</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Disclosure ReadsThe Magenta UFO Case: Italy&#x27;s Alleged 1933 Crash Retrieval, Explained - UAP Disclosure ReadsJuly 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 3, 2026</span></span></span>

According to the narrative popularised by Italian UFO researchers, the Fascist government treated the event as a matter of national security. The alleged reasoning was that the object might represent an advanced secret aircraft rather than an extraterrestrial vehicle. This interpretation fits the wider atmosphere of 1930s military competition, when Italy and other European powers were actively investigating aviation technology, but there is no publicly verified archive record proving that such an investigation occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapdisclosurereads.com/the-magenta-ufo-case-italys-alleged-1933-crash-retrieval-explained/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapdisclosurereads.com">[UAP Disclosure Reads]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapdisclosurereads.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta ufo case italys alleged 1933 crash retrieval explained</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Disclosure ReadsThe Magenta UFO Case: Italy&#x27;s Alleged 1933 Crash Retrieval, Explained - UAP Disclosure ReadsJuly 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 3, 2026</span></span></span>

### 1933–1940s: The claimed RS/33 investigation

A major part of the Magenta story involves a supposed Italian research effort known as [RS/33]({{ 'rs-33/' | relative_url }}), described by later researchers as a secret group created to study the recovered object. Some versions associate the group with Fascist-era scientific and military circles and link it to figures such as Guglielmo Marconi.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://newsroom24.it/notizia/2026/02/25/il-mistero-dellufo-di-mussolini-nel-1933-il-primo-recupero-di-un-ufo-al-mondo" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: newsroom24.it">[Newsroom Italia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">newsroom24.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">il mistero dellufo di mussolini nel 1933 il primo recupero di un ufo al mondo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Newsroom ItaliaIl Mistero dell’UFO di Mussolini, nel 1933 il primo recupero di un Ufo al mondo - Newsroom ItaliaFebruary 25, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: February 25, 2026</span></span></span>

The existence and purpose of this alleged programme remain disputed. The name RS/33 appears frequently in UFO literature, but the public evidence base does not include a confirmed government archive establishing that it was created to analyse a recovered non-human craft. The claim depends heavily on documents and interpretations promoted by later researchers rather than a continuous chain of authenticated wartime records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://uapdisclosurereads.com/the-magenta-ufo-case-italys-alleged-1933-crash-retrieval-explained/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: uapdisclosurereads.com">[UAP Disclosure Reads]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">uapdisclosurereads.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">the magenta ufo case italys alleged 1933 crash retrieval explained</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">UAP Disclosure ReadsThe Magenta UFO Case: Italy&#x27;s Alleged 1933 Crash Retrieval, Explained - UAP Disclosure ReadsJuly 3, 2026...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 3, 2026</span></span></span>

## Claims of wartime movement

### Transfer from Fascist Italy to a secure location

The next stage of the alleged timeline places the object under Italian control for much of the Fascist period. According to accounts later repeated in connection with Grusch’s claims, the Italian government supposedly moved the recovered object to a secure facility and maintained secrecy until the collapse of Mussolini’s regime.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.postapocalypticmedia.com/vatican-ufo/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: postapocalypticmedia.com">[Post Apocalyptic Media]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">postapocalypticmedia.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Post Apocalyptic Media Whistleblower Claims Vatican Helped U.S. Retrieve UFO</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Post Apocalyptic Media Whistleblower Claims Vatican Helped U.S. Retrieve UFO</span></span></span>

### 1944–1945: The alleged US acquisition

The final major stage of the Magenta timeline concerns the transition from Italy to the United States.
