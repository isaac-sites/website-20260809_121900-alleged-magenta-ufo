## Introduction

David Grusch’s 2023 statements became the main modern route by which the alleged Vatican connection to the Magenta UFO crash story entered wider public discussion. A former US intelligence official involved in unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) analysis, Grusch claimed that a 1933 Italian crash-retrieval case was later connected to the United States and that Pope [Pius XII]({{ 'pius-xii/' | relative_url }}) had helped pass information to American officials. His remarks attracted major attention because they linked a decades-old UFO narrative with a prominent historical figure and a wartime diplomatic institution. However, Grusch’s public testimony did not provide documentary proof of a Vatican transfer, a papal communication, or the existence of the alleged object itself.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catholic.org/news/technology/story.php?id=86465" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catholic.org">[Catholic Online]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catholic.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Catholic OnlineIs the Vatican Hiding Evidence of an Alien Crash from 1933? - Technology - News - Catholic OnlineAugust 4, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 4, 2023</span></span></span>

The importance of Grusch’s role is therefore not that he publicly verified the Pius XII story, but that he transformed a niche claim within UFO literature into a widely discussed allegation. His statements created a new public focus on whether the Vatican could have played a hidden intermediary role in the supposed movement of the alleged Magenta object from Fascist Italy to US custody. The central dispute remains the same: Grusch presented the claim as information obtained through intelligence channels, while critics point to the absence of publicly available records that independently confirm the transfer narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

## The 2023 testimony and its main claims

### How Grusch introduced the Vatican connection

The Vatican element emerged during Grusch’s 2023 media appearances and subsequent congressional testimony about alleged government possession of recovered non-human craft. In interviews before his congressional appearance, Grusch described what he said was an early crash-retrieval case in Magenta, Italy, dating to 1933. According to accounts of his remarks, he claimed that information about the alleged recovery was passed through the Vatican, with Pope Pius XII acting as a channel to the United States before the object was eventually acquired by American authorities.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catholic.org/news/technology/story.php?id=86465" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catholic.org">[Catholic Online]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catholic.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Catholic OnlineIs the Vatican Hiding Evidence of an Alien Crash from 1933? - Technology - News - Catholic OnlineAugust 4, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 4, 2023</span></span></span>

The claim was notable because it added a specific historical mechanism to a broader crash-retrieval narrative. Rather than simply alleging that an object existed, the story proposed a chain involving Fascist Italian authorities, the Vatican, and US officials. This made the allegation more historically specific, but it also created more points where evidence would be required: evidence of the crash, evidence of Italian possession, evidence of Vatican involvement, evidence of communication with US officials, and evidence of a later transfer.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.catholic.org/news/technology/story.php?id=86465" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: catholic.org">[Catholic Online]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">catholic.org</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Catholic OnlineIs the Vatican Hiding Evidence of an Alien Crash from 1933? - Technology - News - Catholic OnlineAugust 4, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: August 4, 2023</span></span></span>

At the July 2023 House Oversight Committee hearing, Grusch focused mainly on his broader allegations of a hidden UAP retrieval and reverse-engineering programme. His written statement said he had been informed of a “multi-decade” crash-retrieval programme and that he had spoken with individuals with claimed direct knowledge, but the testimony itself did not publicly introduce [documents]({{ 'documents/' | relative_url }}) proving the Pius XII connection.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

### What Grusch testified under oath

Grusch’s congressional appearance centred on three major claims:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* that the US government had recovered craft of non-human origin;
* that some material had allegedly been subjected to reverse-engineering efforts;
* that information about these activities had not been properly shared with Congress.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

</div>

When questioned publicly, Grusch often distinguished between what he personally knew and what he said he had learned from others. His testimony relied heavily on interviews with people he said had access to classified programmes rather than on publicly available [physical evidence]({{ 'physical-evidence/' | relative_url }}) or released government records.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

That distinction is especially important for the [Vatican claim]({{ 'vatican-claim/' | relative_url }}). A witness saying they were informed of a historical event is different from producing an archive document, diplomatic record, military report, or physical evidence demonstrating that the event occurred. The Pius XII allegation remains dependent on the credibility of the information sources behind Grusch’s statements rather than on independently accessible proof.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

## What was said versus what was documented

The strongest evidence publicly available about Grusch’s Vatican claim is evidence that he made the claim, not evidence that the claimed transfer happened.

There is documentation that Grusch appeared before Congress in July 2023, that he described alleged hidden UAP programmes, and that his testimony received significant attention. The House Oversight Committee officially recorded the hearing and listed Grusch as one of the [witnesses]({{ 'witnesses/' | relative_url }}).<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://oversight.house.gov/hearing/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-implications-on-national-security-public-safety-and-government-transparency/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: oversight.house.gov">[House Oversight Committee]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">oversight.house.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">House Oversight CommitteeUnidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Implications on National Security, Public Safety, and Government Transparency...</span></span></span>

What has not been publicly produced is a confirming record showing that:

* Pope Pius XII received information about a recovered non-human craft in Italy;
* Vatican officials communicated such information to US authorities;
* President Franklin D. Roosevelt or another US official received such a briefing;
* the alleged Magenta object was transferred into American possession.

This [evidence gap]({{ 'evidence-gap/' | relative_url }}) is central to the debate. The claim depends on a series of historical events that would normally be expected to leave traces in government archives, diplomatic correspondence, military records, or personal papers. Supporters argue that classified programmes and wartime secrecy could explain the lack of public documentation, while sceptics argue that extraordinary claims require stronger evidence than testimony about hidden sources.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.defense.gov/2024/Mar/08/2003409233/-1/-1/0/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.defense.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.defense.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">U.S. Department of War AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">U.S. Department of War AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1</span></span></span>(https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3700894/statement-by-pentagon-press-secretary-maj-gen-pat-ryder-on-the-historical-recor/)

The wider US government review of historical UAP claims has also not publicly confirmed the specific chain described by Grusch. The Pentagon’s All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office (AARO) reported in 2024 that it found no empirical evidence that the US government or private companies possessed or reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology. ## Why the claim remains disputed

### A historical connection without a verified record

The Pius XII connection is compelling to some readers because it combines a real historical figure with an alleged secret event. Pope Pius XII was a major diplomatic actor during the Second World War, and the Vatican maintained international channels during the conflict. Those facts explain why the idea of Vatican involvement appears plausible to supporters of the claim. However, a person’s ability to communicate diplomatically does not establish that a particular secret exchange occurred.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2024/05/17/vatican-to-hold-press-conference-on-apparitions-and-other-supernatural-phenomena/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on nypost.com.</span></span></span>

The dispute is therefore not primarily about whether the Vatican had diplomatic contacts. It is about whether those contacts were used for the specific purpose claimed by Grusch. No publicly verified Vatican archive record, US government document, or independent historical study has established that link.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://nypost.com/2024/05/17/vatican-to-hold-press-conference-on-apparitions-and-other-supernatural-phenomena/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nypost.com">[New York Post]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nypost.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open source on nypost.com.</span></span></span>

### The challenge of evaluating second-hand intelligence claims

Grusch’s statements occupy a difficult category in evidence assessment. They are not simply anonymous rumours: they came from a former intelligence official who testified to Congress and described an intelligence process behind his conclusions. That gives the claims public significance. At the same time, many of the most extraordinary details remain based on reported conversations with others rather than released primary evidence.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://openuap.space/record/congress-uap-2023-grusch-statement" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: openuap.space">[Open UAP]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">openuap.space</span><span class="citation-popover-title">congress uap 2023 grusch statement</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Open UAPDavid Grusch — Written Statement (House Oversight UAP Hearing, July 2023) (2023)July 26, 2023...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: July 26, 2023</span></span></span>

For the Magenta and Vatican elements, the key unanswered question is whether future evidence could independently connect the alleged 1933 event, the Vatican, and US acquisition efforts.
