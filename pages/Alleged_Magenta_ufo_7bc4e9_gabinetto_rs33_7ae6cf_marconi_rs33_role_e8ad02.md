## Introduction

Guglielmo Marconi is the strongest historically plausible name in the alleged [Gabinetto]({{ 'rs-33/' | relative_url }}) RS/33 story — and also one of the easiest to overinterpret. In 1933 he genuinely occupied an extraordinary position at the intersection of science and Benito [Mussolini]({{ 'mussolini/' | relative_url }})'s state: he was president of Italy's National Research Council (CNR), president of the Royal Academy of Italy, a senator, a Nobel laureate and an internationally famous authority on radio. By 1932–33, the CNR itself had become the Italian state's principal technical adviser, directly responsible to Mussolini.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-seconda-rivoluzione-scientifica-fisica-e-chimica-organizzazioni-e-autorita-scientifiche_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La seconda rivoluzione scientifica: fisica e chimica. organizzazioni e autorità scientifiche - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>



<figure class="article-media-figure article-media-figure-illustration"><img src="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_marconi_rs33_role_e8ad02-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" alt="Marconi illustration 1" data-theme-src-dark="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_marconi_rs33_role_e8ad02-Illustration-1-dark.svg" | relative_url }}" data-theme-src-light="{{ "/assets/images/Alleged_Magenta_ufo_7bc4e9_gabinetto_rs33_7ae6cf_marconi_rs33_role_e8ad02-Illustration-1-light.svg" | relative_url }}" width="1920" height="1080" loading="lazy" decoding="async" fetchpriority="low"><figcaption class="article-media-caption"><span class="article-media-caption-label">Explanatory illustration 1</span></figcaption></figure>

That makes the claim that Mussolini would turn to Marconi after an extraordinary aerial incident plausible as an institutional scenario. It does **not**, however, demonstrate that he actually headed RS/33. The detailed claim comes primarily from disputed papers circulated anonymously among Italian UFO researchers decades later. No authenticated contemporary appointment, minute, correspondence file or archival record located in the sources examined here independently establishes Marconi as the head of a secret UFO organisation.

The distinction is crucial to the alleged Magenta crash. Marconi's real authority explains why his name makes the RS/33 narrative sound convincing; it cannot substitute for evidence that the organisation existed.

## Marconi really was Mussolini's scientific heavyweight

The strongest part of the RS/33-Marconi story is the part that does not require UFO [documents]({{ 'documents/' | relative_url }}) at all. Marconi was exactly the sort of person whom the Fascist government could have consulted about an [unknown aircraft]({{ 'unknown-aircraft/' | relative_url }}), unusual radio phenomena or strategically important technology.

The official CNR history records Marconi as its president from 1927 until his death in 1937. He had already received the Nobel Prize in Physics in 1909 and become a senator in 1914; in 1930 he became president of the Royal Academy of Italy. Treccani records his adherence to Fascism and emphasises the importance of his CNR presidency within the regime's scientific system.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnr.it/it/presidenti" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnr.it">[CNR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnr.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Presidenti &#124; Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Presidenti &#124; Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche...</span></span></span>

More importantly, his authority was institutional rather than merely ceremonial. A Treccani history of Italian scientific organisation says that in 1932–33 the CNR became the state's highest technical advisory body and answered directly to Mussolini. That places Marconi unusually close to precisely the kind of government decision-making invoked by the RS/33 narrative.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-seconda-rivoluzione-scientifica-fisica-e-chimica-organizzazioni-e-autorita-scientifiche_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La seconda rivoluzione scientifica: fisica e chimica. organizzazioni e autorità scientifiche - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>

There is also documentary evidence that Marconi could operate as an intermediary between scientists and the Fascist leadership. A history of the 1931 international nuclear-physics congress describes a division of labour in which Enrico Fermi handled scientific matters while Marconi dealt with political and formal relations, including securing Mussolini's approval. The same source characterises the Royal Academy as part of Mussolini's ambition to place elite intellectual talent at the state's disposal.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://media.accademiaxl.it/pubblicazioni/ScuolaFermi/allegati/XLROMACONGRESSO.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: media.accademiaxl.it">[media.accademiaxl.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">media.accademiaxl.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il Congresso di Fisica nucleare di Roma del 1931...</span></span></span>

This matters because some modern RS/33 accounts treat Marconi's presence as inherently suspicious or extraordinary. Historically, it is almost the opposite. If Mussolini had wanted a prestigious, politically acceptable scientific figure to supervise an exceptionally sensitive technical investigation in 1933, Marconi would have been an unsurprising candidate.

That observation establishes **historical fit**, not historical fact.

## His real research also resembles parts of the legend

The overlap becomes more striking when Marconi's actual technical interests are considered. During the early 1930s he was working intensively with very short radio wavelengths and microwaves. Treccani records microwave experiments in 1932, including work on communications beyond the optical horizon. The CNR describes his later experiments at Santa Marinella as involving microwave communications, navigation and radio-location — technologies on the path towards radar.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/guglielmo-marconi_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MARCONI, Guglielmo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MARCONI, Guglielmo - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>

Accounts of Italian radar development also connect Marconi's work directly to military interest. The University of Pisa's radar history states that in 1933 Marconi demonstrated to Italian military authorities the detection of disturbances produced when metallic objects crossed a radio beam; this line of investigation helped stimulate Ugo Tiberio's subsequent radar research.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://docenti.ing.unipi.it/labradar/radar.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: docenti.ing.unipi.it">[Ingengeria Università di Pisa]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">docenti.ing.unipi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ingengeria Università di Pisa</span></span></span>

That provides an important alternative explanation for why later stories about Marconi, secret aircraft and strange technology can sound so convincing. He really was experimenting with technologies for detecting objects by radio at approximately the same time that RS/33 was supposedly created.

It does not follow that those experiments originated with recovered technology. Indeed, the documented chronology works without that assumption: radio reflection and detection concepts already had an international prehistory, and specialist historical work cautions against exaggerated claims that Marconi single-handedly created radar in Italy.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://art.torvergata.it/bitstream/2108/96787/1/RA141363.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: art.torvergata.it">[art.torvergata.it]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">art.torvergata.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Proceedings of the 11th European Radar Conference</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">October 11, 2024...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: October 11, 2024</span></span></span>

The same caution applies to stories about Marconi's supposed "[death ray]({{ 'death-ray/' | relative_url }})". Later family recollections and popular accounts describe experiments intended to stop engines, while modern treatments have sometimes folded that legend into RS/33. But the claimed connection between such a weapon and recovered UFO technology rests on documents themselves described in contemporary reporting as being of highly doubtful origin.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.fanpage.it/innovazione/tecnologia/il-raggio-della-morte-cosa-sappiamo-sullarma-e-sulla-sua-esistenza/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fanpage.it">[Fanpage]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fanpage.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il Raggio della Morte: cosa sappiamo sull&#x27;arma e sulla sua</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il Raggio della Morte: cosa sappiamo sull&#x27;arma e sulla sua...May 21, 2024 — 21 May 2024 — Una serie di documenti dall&#x27;origine più...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 21, 2024</span></span></span>

In other words, authentic Marconi research supplies the RS/33 narrative with unusually convincing historical scenery. It does not authenticate the narrative.

## What the RS/33 material actually says about Marconi

The more specific story comes from documents and research publicised by Italian UFO investigators Alfredo Lissoni and Roberto [Pinotti]({{ 'pinotti/' | relative_url }}). In that version, Marconi was selected to lead Gabinetto RS/33 after Mussolini's government confronted reports of unconventional aircraft in 1933.

Lissoni's published account goes further than simply attaching Marconi's name to the organisation. It argues that several alleged RS/33 scientists had genuine connections with established Italian scientific institutions and highlights Marconi's presidency of the CNR as part of the pattern. The argument is essentially cumulative: the named individuals occupied positions that would make such a committee conceivable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ecn.org/cunfi/rs33.htm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ecn.org">[ecn.org]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ecn.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il Gabinetto RS-33</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il Gabinetto RS-33</span></span></span>

Yet the same body of material introduces a curious qualification. One circulated version describes Marconi as the **nominal** head of RS/33 but says that he was effectively absent, with practical work conducted through others. This significantly weakens the popular image of Marconi personally leading a laboratory that was reverse-engineering the Magenta object. The allegation is closer to an eminent figurehead presiding over a compartmented committee than to the hands-on director sometimes portrayed in modern retellings.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.silverland.info/documenti/gabinetto-rs-33/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silverland.info">[Il mondo degli UFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silverland.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il mondo degli UFOGabinetto RS/33</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il mondo degli UFO...</span></span></span>

There is another important distinction. The early RS/33 material does not consistently present the committee as having immediately concluded that extraterrestrials were involved. Its proponents describe disagreement over whether unusual aircraft might instead represent advanced British, French or German technology. That is much more compatible with the security concerns of 1930s Europe than the modern shorthand of "Mussolini's alien reverse-engineering programme".<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.silverland.info/documenti/gabinetto-rs-33/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: silverland.info">[Il mondo degli UFO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">silverland.info</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Il mondo degli UFOGabinetto RS/33</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Il mondo degli UFO...</span></span></span>

So even if the disputed papers were provisionally accepted at face value, the strongest version of the Marconi story would still need qualification: alleged chairman, uncertain degree of personal involvement, uncertain interpretation of the objects, and no demonstrated programme of extraterrestrial reverse engineering.

## The Enrico Fermi connection needs particular caution

Enrico Fermi is frequently added to the RS/33 story as another scientific heavyweight supposedly serving beneath Marconi. That pairing sounds formidable because the two men genuinely knew one another and operated within overlapping elite scientific institutions.

Their documented relationship, however, is perfectly explicable without RS/33. Fermi was already an Italian academician, while Marconi occupied senior institutional and political positions. At the 1931 nuclear-physics congress, Fermi served as secretary-general and Marconi as honorary president; archival research describes Marconi handling relations with the regime while Fermi concentrated on scientific organisation.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/enrico-fermi_%28Il-Contributo-italiano-alla-storia-del-Pensiero%3A-Scienze%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fermi, Enrico</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fermi, Enrico - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>

That genuine relationship creates another trap for assessing the RS/33 papers. A document or narrative that places Marconi and Fermi in the same institutional orbit has not necessarily revealed obscure information. Their association belonged to the documented scientific establishment of Fascist Italy.

This is a recurring credibility problem with the alleged [RS/33 membership lists]({{ 'member-list/' | relative_url }}): historically correct relationships can demonstrate that a story's author knew the period, but they cannot establish that the secret organisation being described was real.

## A Marconi descendant later endorsed the story

The most notable evidence outside the original UFO-document circle appeared in April 2024. In an interview with Italian magazine *Oggi*, Marconi's daughter Elettra and her son Guglielmo discussed his life and legacy. The interviewer directly asked whether Mussolini had put Marconi in charge of a commission investigating UFOs.

Guglielmo answered yes, identified it as "Ricerche Speciali 33", said that Fermi was under Marconi, and added that his grandfather believed extraterrestrial life existed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.oggi.it/attualita/notizie/2024/04/27/guglielmo-marconi-la-figlia-elettra-mio-papa-invento-il-futuro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: oggi.it">[Oggi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">oggi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span></span></span>

This testimony deserves to be recorded because it is a direct, public family claim rather than an anonymous internet retelling. But its evidential limits are substantial. The interview was published in 2024, roughly 87 years after Marconi's death. The answer supplies no appointment document, diary entry, contemporary letter or other independently testable provenance. Nor does the article explain how the family learned about RS/33 or whether that knowledge predates the public circulation of the RS/33 story in UFO literature.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.oggi.it/attualita/notizie/2024/04/27/guglielmo-marconi-la-figlia-elettra-mio-papa-invento-il-futuro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: oggi.it">[Oggi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">oggi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span></span></span>

The distinction between family testimony and contemporary evidence is particularly important here. The same interview contains several dramatic family traditions about Marconi destroying experiments and developing an engine-stopping "blocking ray". Such recollections are historically interesting, but they require independent documentation before they can establish secret government programmes.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.oggi.it/attualita/notizie/2024/04/27/guglielmo-marconi-la-figlia-elettra-mio-papa-invento-il-futuro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: oggi.it">[Oggi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">oggi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span></span></span>

The 2024 statement therefore strengthens the case that the RS/33 story has entered Marconi family tradition. It does not independently authenticate the alleged 1933 organisation.

## The archival problem remains decisive

For a claim involving one of the most prominent scientists in Fascist Italy, Marconi left a substantial documentary footprint. His surviving records are not confined to a single collection. The Fondazione Guglielmo Marconi identifies the large Marconi Archives at Oxford's Bodleian Library, while its own inventories encompass additional Marconi-related collections. Official Italian archival material also preserves records of the institutions he headed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://fondazioneguglielmomarconi.it/fondi-e-inventari/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: fondazioneguglielmomarconi.it">[Fondazione Guglielmo Marconi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">fondazioneguglielmomarconi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Fondazione Guglielmo Marconi Fondi e inventari</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Fondazione Guglielmo Marconi Fondi e inventari</span></span></span>

The Royal Academy of Italy's published archival inventory illustrates the level of documentation available for Marconi's ordinary institutional activities. For 1933 alone it records secret academy meetings, scientific-class proceedings and even a letter transmitting material from Marconi to Mussolini.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.lincei.it/sites/default/files/attachments/Inventario_Reale_Accademia_d_Italia.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: lincei.it">[Accademia Dei Lincei]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">lincei.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Inventario Reale Accademia d Italia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Inventario Reale Accademia d Italia</span></span></span>

That does not prove that every clandestine activity would have survived on paper. Fascist Italy maintained secrets, records could be destroyed, and Marconi's family has itself described him as protective of unpublished research. Absence from surviving archives therefore cannot logically prove that RS/33 never existed.

But it changes the burden of evidence. When an alleged organisation is known chiefly through anonymously supplied documents, while authenticated archives document the same central figure's real scientific and political work in considerable detail, the anonymous material requires especially strong authentication.

Historical plausibility cannot repair missing provenance.

## Why Marconi makes RS/33 sound more credible than it is

Marconi's name performs several jobs simultaneously within the alleged Magenta story. He supplies world-class scientific expertise, direct access to Mussolini, knowledge of radio and detection technology, political acceptability to the regime and documented relationships with scientists such as Fermi. Almost every institutional requirement of a hypothetical 1933 investigation can therefore be mapped onto something Marconi genuinely possessed.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-seconda-rivoluzione-scientifica-fisica-e-chimica-organizzazioni-e-autorita-scientifiche_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La seconda rivoluzione scientifica: fisica e chimica. organizzazioni e autorità scientifiche - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>

That is precisely why his alleged membership needs to be evaluated more strictly, not less strictly.

A useful way to separate the evidence is to distinguish three levels of claim:


<div class="content-enhancement content-enhancement--insight-grid" markdown="1">

* **Well documented:** Marconi was a Nobel laureate, senator, Fascist adherent, CNR president and Royal Academy president with access to Mussolini and major influence over Italian science.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cnr.it/it/presidenti" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cnr.it">[CNR]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cnr.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Presidenti &#124; Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Presidenti &#124; Consiglio Nazionale delle Ricerche...</span></span></span>
* **Historically plausible:** Mussolini could reasonably have consulted Marconi about an unknown aircraft, radio phenomenon or strategically important technology. Marconi's documented microwave and radio-location research makes such consultation particularly conceivable.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/guglielmo-marconi_%28Dizionario-Biografico%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">MARCONI, Guglielmo</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">MARCONI, Guglielmo - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>
* **Unverified:** Marconi headed an organisation called Gabinetto RS/33 that investigated the alleged Magenta craft, supervised Fermi in that programme or directed research into extraterrestrial technology. Those propositions still depend on disputed material and later testimony rather than authenticated contemporary records.

</div>

This separation explains why arguments over Marconi often become confused. Evidence supporting the first category is sometimes presented as though it proves the third.

## Historical fit is not proof of RS/33 membership

Marconi is not an obviously anachronistic addition to the RS/33 story. Quite the contrary: his real career fits the alleged role remarkably well. In 1933 he occupied the summit of Italian scientific administration, possessed Mussolini's political confidence, knew many of the scientists later attached to the story and was conducting research close enough to radio detection and advanced communications to make an investigation of mysterious aircraft seem entirely within his professional world.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/la-seconda-rivoluzione-scientifica-fisica-e-chimica-organizzazioni-e-autorita-scientifiche_%28Storia-della-Scienza%29/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: treccani.it">[Treccani]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">treccani.it</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">La seconda rivoluzione scientifica: fisica e chimica. organizzazioni e autorità scientifiche - Enciclopedia - Treccani...</span></span></span>

That is the strongest case **for the plausibility** of his alleged role.

The strongest case **against treating the claim as established history** is evidential rather than biographical. The detailed RS/33 narrative surfaced through anonymously supplied documents many decades after the supposed events. The historical record independently verifies Marconi's genuine offices, research, contacts and dealings with Mussolini, but the sources examined here do not provide a comparably authenticated contemporary record appointing him to RS/33.

The 2024 statement by his descendant is noteworthy but cannot close that gap.<span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.oggi.it/attualita/notizie/2024/04/27/guglielmo-marconi-la-figlia-elettra-mio-papa-invento-il-futuro" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: oggi.it">[Oggi]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">oggi.it</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Guglielmo Marconi, inventore della radio, raccontato dalla figlia Eelettra</span></span></span>

Marconi therefore remains central to the [alleged Magenta UFO]({{ 'alleged-magenta-ufo/' | relative_url }}) crash for an unusual reason: he demonstrates how effectively a disputed secret-history claim can fit genuine history. His career shows that a Mussolini-appointed scientific investigation headed by Marconi would have been institutionally possible. What remains unproved is the much more consequential proposition that Gabinetto RS/33 was that investigation — and that Marconi actually led it.
